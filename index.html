PRIVATE SELLER TOOL

StarNet POD Manager

StarNet POD Manager is a private tool used by the owner of an Etsy shop to organize print-on-demand product research, original design development, product listing preparation, and listing management.

Purpose

The tool is intended for use by the shop owner and authorized collaborators. It aims to streamline routine seller workflows while maintaining appropriate review of intellectual property, product information, and marketplace requirements.

Data and access

The application is intended to access only the Etsy shop data needed for its seller-management features, subject to the permissions granted by the shop owner. It is not a public marketplace or a service for other Etsy sellers.
